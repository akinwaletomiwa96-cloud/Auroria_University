More FAQs →
How do I apply to Auroria?

Create an account, explore programmes, select a course, complete the application form, and review your details before submitting.

Can I track my application?

Sign in and open your dashboard or My Applications page to see your application reference and status.

Where can I find entry requirements?

Visit the Admissions page or open a course's details to review the requirements for that programme.

Do I need an account to apply?

Yes. Create an account or log in first so your application can be linked to your user profile.
