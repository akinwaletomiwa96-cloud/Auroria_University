03 BEFORE YOU APPLY

Know what
you'll need.

Your chosen programme determines the qualifications and supporting information you'll need. Use these general guidelines as a starting point, then check the requirements listed for your course.

01 / UNDERGRADUATE
↗

Starting your first degree

Undergraduate applicants are generally expected to provide evidence of completed secondary education or an accepted equivalent qualification.

  • Secondary-school examination results
  • Required subjects and grades for the chosen course
  • Valid contact and personal information
  • Additional evidence where requested
Check your course first ↗
02 / POSTGRADUATE
✳

Taking your studies further

Postgraduate applicants generally need an appropriate recognised undergraduate qualification relevant to their intended programme.

  • Bachelor's degree certificate or evidence of award
  • Academic transcripts
  • Relevant experience, where requested
  • Personal statement or other programme-specific documents
Requirements vary by programme ↗
03 / INTERNATIONAL
◎

Applying with international qualifications

If you studied outside the university's usual education system, provide clear information about your qualification and the institution that awarded it.

  • Examination certificates or official results
  • Transcripts or subject-level records, if applicable
  • Translations where documents are not accepted in their original language
  • Additional verification or language evidence, if required

06 GOOD TO KNOW

Questions worth
asking.

A few answers to help you prepare before starting your application.

Still unsure about something? Contact us ↗
Can I apply before receiving my final results?

This depends on the programme's admissions rules. Some institutions allow applications with pending results, while others require completed qualifications. Check the specific requirements before applying.

What if my qualification is not listed?

Do not assume that your qualification will be rejected. Contact the admissions team and provide the name of the qualification, awarding institution and available results so that its suitability can be assessed.

Can I change my chosen course after applying?

Changes may be possible, but they depend on the university's process and the availability and entry requirements of the alternative course. Contact the admissions team before making changes.

How do I know whether my application was submitted?

Look for a submission confirmation or application reference after completing the form. If the website provides an application dashboard, log in to check the information recorded there.

Does submitting an application guarantee admission?

No. An application must be reviewed against the relevant entry requirements and admissions process. Make sure all information you provide is accurate and respond to any requests for further information.

Where can I find tuition fees for my course?

Visit the course catalogue to view the estimated fees displayed for each programme. Confirm the applicable fees and payment arrangements with the university before making financial commitments.
