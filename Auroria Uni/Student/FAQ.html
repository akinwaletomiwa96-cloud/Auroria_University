Good questions.
Clear answers.

We've organised some common questions to help you find the information you're looking for without the extra steps.

⌕ SEARCH

Start by exploring the available courses. When you are ready, create an account through the registration page, sign in and follow the instructions for the admission application. Review the information you enter before submitting.

Requirements can vary by programme and entry route. Visit the Admissions page for the requirements presented by Auroria University and check that you meet the criteria for your chosen course before applying.

Open the Courses page to browse the available programmes. Use the course search and department filters, where available, to narrow the list. Read each course description and review its entry requirements and estimated fees.

Check the Courses page and the relevant course details for the estimated fees displayed on the website. Confirm any final fee information with the university before making financial decisions.

Register for an account and sign in. Open the admission application area, complete the requested fields, review your details and submit the form. Follow any confirmation displayed by the website.

Sign in using the account associated with your application and open your student dashboard or application information page. The information available depends on the functionality enabled on the website.

Check that you have completed the required fields and that your information is correct. If the problem continues, use the contact form below to describe the issue. Do not include your password in a message.

Select the registration option and complete the required fields, including your name, email address, phone number and password. Follow the website's validation instructions and submit the form to create your account.

Check your email address and password, then try again. If the website provides a password recovery feature, follow its instructions. Otherwise, contact the university through the enquiry form for assistance.

Explore the Campus Life page for information about student communities, recreational activities, facilities, events and opportunities to connect with other students. Specific activities depend on what the university offers.

Visit the Campus Life page to explore the facilities and student experiences described on the website. For details about a particular facility, use the contact form to make an enquiry.

Use the contact section on this page to prepare an enquiry. The university's actual email address, phone number and physical address should be added to the contact details once they have been confirmed.
